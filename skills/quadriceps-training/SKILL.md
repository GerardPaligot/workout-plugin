# Quadriceps training

## Anatomy and main action

The quadriceps is made up of 4 muscle heads (vastus lateralis, vastus medialis, vastus intermedius, rectus femoris). Their main action is **knee extension**.

### Key to targeting: knee flexion > hip flexion

To target the quadriceps effectively, you need to maximize **knee flexion** in the exercise. If an exercise mostly causes large hip flexion, it will mainly target the glutes and posterior chain, not the quadriceps.

## Exercise ranking (worst to best)

### Avoid

#### Circular leg press / V-squat
- The circular path imposes **very large hip flexion** and insufficient knee flexion.
- Mainly works the glutes and posterior chain, not the quadriceps.
- Popular because you can load a lot of weight (ego lifting: the glutes get massively involved).
- **Nuance**: can be suitable if you have very short femurs + excellent hip mobility, but that is a minority of lifters.

#### Linear leg press
- Same problems as the circular one to a lesser degree: insufficient range of motion for the quadriceps, too much hip flexion.
- Without the right morphological conditions (short femurs, good hip mobility), there is not much to gain for the quadriceps.
- Loading very heavy is tempting but unproductive → time wasted loading/unloading plates.
- **Interesting variation**: "cannonball" press (heels off the platform, knees brought to the chest) → maximizes knee flexion → the only variation potentially relevant for the quadriceps.

#### Squat (barbell on the back)
- Is **not the best** exercise for the quadriceps despite its popularity.
- Technically very hard to master, insufficient stability.
- Loads many muscle groups → hard to isolate the quadriceps, especially with long femurs.
- To make it a "full quadriceps" variation, you need a **heel wedge** (10-15 cm) or **weightlifting shoes** to maximize knee flexion.
- **Still recommended** for: improving stability, overall lower-body work, general performance. But it is not an optimal choice if the goal is 100% quadriceps.

### Very good exercises

#### Leg extension (with a good machine)
- Works the quadriceps in their **main action**: knee extension.
- Excellent **stability**, easy progressive overload.
- **Warning**: not all machines are equal. The resistance curve and the range of knee flexion vary by brand.
- The best machines have **variable resistance** (adapted cam), which changes everything about the stimulation.
- With the right machine, you could do a complete quadriceps session on it alone.

#### Walking lunges (dumbbells in hands)
- **Easier** to master than the squat: no bar on the back, better grasp of the movement.
- Surprisingly good stability (dumbbells in the hands = lower center of gravity).
- **Adaptable to any morphology**: change the step length to target the quadriceps more or less (short steps = more quadriceps).
- Changing step length is much simpler than putting a wedge under the heels for the squat.
- If you only do one exercise for the whole lower body → walking lunges.

### Best exercise

#### Linear hack squat
- **The best exercise** for the quadriceps. Ticks every box:
  - **Linear path** → easily adaptable to any morphology (adjustable foot position).
  - Good **resistance curve**, which can be improved with resistance bands.
  - **Maximum stability**: machine with a predefined travel axis, backrest, shoulder pads.
  - Easy **progressive overload**.
- The angle can vary by brand, but all linear hack squats are pretty good.
- **Warning**: don't confuse it with the **circular** hack squat (to be avoided) or with the **pendulum squat** (good exercise but progressive overload is harder because of the machine's architecture).
- Widely used by professional bodybuilders (Ronnie Coleman, Chris Bumstead...).
- Machine availability varies by gym; it is more common in some regions and still spreading in others.

## Exercises not covered above but valid

These exercises work the quadriceps but are inferior to the hack squat:
- **Zercher squat**, **sissy squat**, **Bulgarian split squats**: valid in certain contexts, but clearly inferior to the hack squat for targeting the quadriceps.

## Minimal quadriceps program

| Exercise | Type | Sets |
|---|---|---|
| Linear hack squat | Compound, optimal path | 3-4 |
| Walking lunges | Compound, adaptable | 3-4 |
| Leg extension (good machine) | Single-joint, main action | 3-4 |
